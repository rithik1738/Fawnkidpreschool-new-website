VISIONARIES

The Minds Behind Fawn Kids

Meet our founders who combined art, science, and the Montessori method to create a revolutionary learning space.

Founder & Director

Mangala Priya B.V.

An accomplished artist and an MBA graduate with a Diploma in Montessori Education, Mangala Priya envisions a learning space where a child's exploration knows no bounds. With a special interest in Early Childhood Psychology and Fine Arts, she focuses on nurturing the "whole child." She believes that education in the early years should not be rigid, but a joyful canvas where children are free to discover their true potential.

"Creativity is the heartbeat of education."
Co-Founder & Academic Head

Divya Devi B.V.

Holding a Master’s degree in Biotechnology and bringing years of experience as an IB educator, Divya Devi brings a research-backed, analytical approach to early learning. Specializing in Montessori Curriculum design and Creative Pedagogy, she ensures that every activity at Fawn Kids is purpose-built. Her expertise transforms our classrooms into spaces where scientific curiosity and joyful learning go hand-in-hand.

"Blending scientific inquiry with artistic expression."
