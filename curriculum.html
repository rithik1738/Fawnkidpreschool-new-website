OUR MINDSET

The Core Philosophy

Brain development is most rapid during the early years. By age 7, a child has already developed 75% of their neural connectivity.

Ages 2-6

The critical window for acquiring fundamental cognitive and social frameworks.

Intelligence

We differentiate between Inherent and Acquired Intelligence through tailored exploration.

LEVEL 01

Playgroup

Sensory Exploration

The first step away from home focuses on providing a safe, nurturing environment. Children build trust while exploring the world through their senses and developing early motor skills.

  • Tactile exploration and color recognition
  • Gross and fine motor skill development
  • Building trust and independence
LEVEL 02

Nursery

Social & Emotional Growth

As curiosity grows, the Nursery curriculum shifts towards social interaction and language acquisition. We emphasize emotional regulation, sharing, and communication.

  • Social and emotional expression
  • Language enrichment through song and story
  • Group play and cooperation
LEVEL 03

LKG

Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

The Lower Kindergarten stage introduces structured concepts through Montessori apparatus. Children begin to bridge the gap between concrete objects and abstract symbols in math and language.

  • Introduction to Phonics and letter formation
  • Concrete understanding of numbers up to 100
  • Botanical and zoological naming exercises
LEVEL 04

UKG

Advanced Cognitive & Preparatory

In the final preschool year, we prepare children for primary school. The curriculum integrates complex arithmetic, advanced literacy, and an introduction to world cultures.

  • Sentence construction and early creative writing
  • Mathematical operations using the Golden Bead material
  • Preparation for mainstream primary school entry
