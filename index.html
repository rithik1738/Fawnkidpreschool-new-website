FAQs

ASK A QUESTIONS FOR DOUBTS

1. What is the teaching methodology followed at Fawn Kids Academy?
We follow a specialized Montessori-inspired curriculum powered by Kreedo Early Childhood Solutions. Our approach is play-based and child-centric, focusing on hands-on experiential learning rather than rote memorization. We provide scientifically backed learning materials that help children develop cognitive, motor, and social skills at their own pace.
2. What are the age criteria for different programs?
We offer structured programs tailored for early childhood development:

Playgroup: 3+ Years
Nursery (Pre-KG): 3 to 4 Years
LKG: 4 to 5 Years
UKG: 5 to 6 Years
3. Why is early education so important for my child?
Research shows that 75% of a child's neural connectivity is established by the age of 7. The ages between 2 and 6 are the peak years for absorbing new concepts. We provide the right environment, stimulation, and tools to maximize both your child's inherent and acquired intelligence during this critical window.
4. Who are the educators guiding my child?
Our leadership team and educators bring a unique blend of expertise. Guided by our founders, who hold qualifications in Montessori Education, Fine Arts, and Science, our teachers act as inspiring role models. They focus on nurturing every child's creativity, independence, and foundational learning.
5. How can I enroll my child or schedule a campus visit?
We would love to welcome you and your child! You can click the "Enroll Now" button on our website, call us directly at 9445566377 / 9499909005, or drop an email to fawnkidspreschool@gmail.com to schedule a campus tour and discuss the admission process.
