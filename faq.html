Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Fawn Kids Academy in one place.

1. Admissions & Enrollment

How do I enroll my child at Fawn Kids Academy?
Enrollment is simple. Start by filling out our online application, or visit our Tondiarpet campus for a personal tour and enrollment consultation.
What are the age requirements?
We offer tailored programs for Playgroup (2+ Years), Nursery/Pre-KG (3+ Years), LKG (4+ Years), and UKG (5+ Years).
Do you offer campus tours?
Yes! Campus tours are available Monday to Friday (9 AM - 4 PM) and Saturdays by appointment.

2. Curriculum & Learning

What is the teaching approach?
We use a child-centered approach powered by Kreedo Early Childhood Solutions, blending Montessori principles with modern pedagogy.
How do you handle transitions from home to school?
We have a gentle settling-in process where educators work closely with parents to make the child feel safe and excited.
How are parents kept informed?
Parents receive regular updates, milestone tracking reports, and invitations to PTMs to discuss holistic development.

3. Campus & Facilities

What are the school timings?
Generally 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM for early years, with extended hours for older groups.
What safety measures are in place?
Secure entry points, CCTV monitoring, child-safe furniture, and staff trained in first aid.
