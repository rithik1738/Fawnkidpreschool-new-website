Lower Kindergarten (LKG)

A transformative year where curiosity meets structured learning. We nurture independent thinkers, preparing them for the academic journey ahead with joy and confidence.

Apply Now Book a Tour

Fostering Independence & Joy.

Our LKG program is designed to bridge the gap between playful exploration and structured academic foundation. We focus on developing core cognitive skills, early literacy, and emotional intelligence in a highly supportive environment.

Through the renowned Kreedo curriculum, children engage with specialized materials that make abstract concepts tangible. They don't just learn; they experience the joy of discovery.

What They Learn

Language

Phonics, early reading strategies, and confident verbal expression through storytelling and role-play.

Logic

Understanding numbers, patterns, spatial relationships, and foundational mathematical thinking.

Sensorial

Refining the senses to classify, compare, and understand the physical properties of the world.

Creativity

Unrestricted artistic expression, music, and imaginative play to foster out-of-the-box thinking.
